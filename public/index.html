About Safe Harbor

Safe Harbor is a volunteer-run directory connecting elderly people and women facing homelessness with shelter, food, health care, and legal support in Springfield. Every listing is checked by a local volunteer. This project is open source — contributions and local resource updates are welcome.

Safe Harbor · If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.
